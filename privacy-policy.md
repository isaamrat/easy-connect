Easy Connect is a client-side Chrome extension designed for BRACU faculty members.
1. Data Processing: All advising validation, grade summary calculations, and attendance operations occur locally on the user's computer.
2. Data Collection: Easy Connect does not collect, track, transmit, or store any personal data or student records on any remote servers.
3. Third Parties: No data is shared with or sold to third parties.
4. Permissions: Permissions are used strictly to communicate with connect.bracu.ac.bd in real time during user-initiated actions.
