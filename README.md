Easy Connect is a productivity extension designed exclusively for BRACU faculty members to streamline routine portal tasks on connect.bracu.ac.bd.

KEY FEATURES:

• Student Advising Validation: Instant student panel check with automated prerequisite verification, overlap course restrictions, and live schedule clash detection.
• Batch Advising Validation: Validate entire lists of students at once to speed through peak advising periods with automated multi-student processing.
• Dedicated Advising Batch Viewer: A full-screen interactive workspace featuring search, filtering, and detailed student-by-student validation summaries.
• Single Grade Sheet Lookup: Instant access to organized semester-by-semester course histories, credits, and CGPA metrics directly in your side panel.
• Batch Grade Fetcher: Retrieve academic records for multiple students simultaneously with live progress tracking and bulk export options.
• Dedicated Interactive Grade Viewer: A full-page, clean grade report viewer with comprehensive semester tables, GPA overviews, and easy export.
• Exam Attendance Downloader: Download all sections exam attendance sheets as a single compiled ZIP archive in one click.
• Seamless Side Panel UI: Operates side-by-side with your active browser window without interrupting or obscuring your workflow.

PRIVACY & SECURITY:
• 100% Client-Side: All validation calculations, summaries, and viewers run securely within your browser.
• No Third-Party Tracking: Does not collect, transmit, or sell personal student or faculty data.
• Official Portal Only: Active solely on connect.bracu.ac.bd.
